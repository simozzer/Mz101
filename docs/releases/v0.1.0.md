The first release of **Mz101**, an emulation of the Roland SH-101 monophonic synthesizer, as a VST3 instrument and a standalone app for Windows.

![The Mz101 panel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simozzer/Mz101/v0.1.0/docs/screenshot.png)

Mz101 is modelled on the original circuit, using Roland's 1982 service manual:
- **Oscillator:** a CEM3340-style core, with saw, pulse and a phase-locked sub oscillator all playing at once.
- **Filter:** an IR3109 24 dB filter with the 101's resonance circuit. It saturates, thins the bass as resonance rises, and whistles in tune at the top.
- **Envelope:** snappy analog curves.
- **The 101's quirks:** pitch drift, power rail sag, and the CPU's slightly loose timing.

**Everything the hardware has:** arpeggiator, 100-step sequencer with rests and ties, portamento (off/auto/on), key transpose, and pitch and filter bend.

**Added for DAW use:**
- **Sync:** the sequencer, arpeggiator and LFO can each follow your project's tempo.
- **MIDI control:** every control has a MIDI CC, and you can MIDI-learn your own.
- **Hover hints** on every control.
- **Ten factory patches.**

### Download

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Mz101-0.1.0-win64-VST3.zip` | The plugin. Copy `Mz101.vst3` to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Common\VST3` (or `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3`) and rescan in your DAW. |
| `Mz101-0.1.0-win64-Standalone.zip` | `Mz101.exe`, which plays without a DAW. |

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The binaries aren't code-signed, so Windows may warn about an unknown publisher. Choose *More info → Run anyway*, or build from source.

### Known limitations
- **Windows only** for now.
- **Not calibrated against a real SH-101.** Ranges follow Roland's specifications, but some internal levels and curves are worked out from the circuit, not measured from a unit.
- **Tuned to A4 = 440 Hz.** The original was calibrated to 442 Hz; TUNE covers the difference.
